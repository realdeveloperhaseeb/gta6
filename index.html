About the game ↓

Play GTA 6 Lite online — free, no download

GTA 6 Lite is a fan-made, open-world action game that runs entirely in your web browser. You play as Haseeb, newly arrived in the sunny coastal city of Palmera Bay, working his way up through six story missions: stealing supercars, clearing a gang out of a nightclub, raiding a police station, breaking into an army base and finishing with a fighter-jet victory lap.

Everything is built in a single page with hand-written WebGL: real-time shadows, a full day and night cycle, reflective water, neon nightlife, explosions and a live synthesised soundtrack. Click Play now and you are in the city in seconds.

Features

Open-world city

Drive, walk or fly anywhere in Palmera Bay: downtown towers, parks, a beach road, a dockside and a fenced army base.

Enter buildings

Walk into Club Velvet's dance floor or the Palmera Bay police station for indoor missions.

Real cops and soldiers

A five-star wanted system with police cars, officers, a police helicopter at four stars, plus army guards.

Tanks, helicopters, jets

Crush cars in a battle tank, fire rockets from an attack helicopter and take off in a fighter jet.

Weapons

Pistol, SMG, assault rifle, shotgun, sniper rifle with scope, minigun, grenade launcher, RPG and grenades, with fast reloads.

Share your stats

Every mission gives a star rating with time, takedowns and accuracy. Share to WhatsApp, Facebook, X or Telegram.

25 story missions

Prologue, Ten Years Earlier: teenage Haseeb, his best friend Omar and his first love Zara. Act I, Welcome to Palmera Bay: Haseeb arrives and works his way up. Act II, Streets of Fire: the Viper gang's new boss, Kade, starts a war. Act III, Red Tide: a private army invades the city by land, air and sea. Act IV, The Agent: spy work for Agent Nadia Cross and a race to rescue Zara. Act V, Blood Money: a bank heist, a street race in the rain, and a betrayal from inside the family. Act VI, Kingdom: a police station jailbreak, a nightclub shootout, an air war and the hunt for the man behind Red Tide, Victor Kane.

Side jobs, weather and secrets

Between story missions, Palmera Bay keeps you busy: three street races against the clock (Downtown Sprint, Coast Run, Night Rider), three rampages (SMG, minigun and a bare-knuckle brawl) and 24 hidden packages glowing green around the city. The weather changes on its own from clear skies to overcast, rain and full thunderstorms with lightning, wet reflective roads and rain on the screen.
